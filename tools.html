Snowflake Credit & Cost Estimator
Estimate monthly Snowflake spend from warehouse size, active hours, idle time and storage, and see what a 1-minute auto-suspend would save.

        

        

        
Example prices only. Credit and storage rates depend on your edition, region and contract; edit them to match your account. Larger warehouses finish queries faster, so for queries that scale well they often cost about the same per query.

AWS Savings Estimator
Quantify savings from stopping non-production on a schedule, commitments, gp2 to gp3 storage and snapshot cleanup. Enter your own rates.

        

        
Rates shown are examples; check current AWS pricing for your Region and service. Do not buy a 24/7 commitment for capacity you plan to stop on a schedule: choose one or the other for the same instances.
